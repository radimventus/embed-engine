# CAP-P01 Validation — Better Decision Than Photos Alone?

## Question

Can a buyer reach a **better decision** about Modern 01 than by browsing photos alone?

## Answer

**Yes — if the Layout Story is followed.**

### Why photos alone fail

- Exterior and hero shots sell atmosphere, not morning bathroom queues.  
- A large living room photo hides a 14 m² kitchen trade-off.  
- Stairs and zone splits are invisible in beauty frames.  
- Buyers attach emotionally before constraints are named.

### Why this Pilot knowledge model helps

1. **Object Package** states rooms, zones, areas, and honest weaknesses.  
2. **Behavior Pack** forces explain / warn / compare / ask before recommend.  
3. **Moves** require spatial Signals (room/floor/floorplan) — evidence, not vibes.  
4. **Story** ends in an explicit disposition fit conclusion.  
5. **Terminal + peers** keep one mind (Interpretation) so Priority/FAQ/AI reinforce the same layout focus.

### Decision quality test (pass criteria)

A buyer can answer without guessing:

- Day/night fit for our life?  
- Kitchen/dining compromise acceptable?  
- One upstairs bath OK?  
- Stairs OK?  
- Therefore pursue / conditional / no on **layout** grounds?

If yes, CAP-P01 product goal is met — even before Strategy/Terminal are fully implemented in code.
