# Platform Architecture 1.0 (Working Draft)

## Addendum -- Identity, Perspective & Persistent Decision Identity

# Identity Layer

The platform distinguishes between Identity, Permissions, Perspective
and Terminal.

Identity ↓ Permissions ↓ Perspective ↓ Terminal ↓ Runtime

Identity answers: Who is interacting with the platform?

Permissions answer: What is this identity allowed to access?

Perspective answers: Which question is this identity asking the Runtime?

Terminal is the Runtime projection optimized for that Perspective.

# Persistent Decision Identity

Decision Session is transient.

Persistent Decision Identity is continuous.

One identity owns multiple Decision Sessions.

Persistent Decision Identity ├─ Decision Session 1 ├─ Decision Session 2
├─ Decision Session 3 └─ Decision Session N

# Decision Journey

Persistent Decision Identity ↓ Decision Journey ↓ Decision Sessions ↓
Stories ↓ Outcomes

# Sales Value

Persistent identity enables: - returning visitor recognition -
cross-device continuity - long-term priority evolution - AI
recommendations from history - stronger sales signals - personalized
follow-up

# Registration Philosophy

Registration should preserve continuity, not merely create an account.

Suggested messaging: - Continue your decision later. - Save your
Decision Journey. - Keep your priorities and comparisons.

# Architectural Invariants

1.  Runtime remains the only author of semantic meaning.
2.  Identity stores continuity, not interpretation.
3.  Decision Sessions are transient.
4.  Decision Journeys are persistent.
5.  Perspectives define Runtime projections.
6.  Terminals visualize Perspectives.
7.  Registration preserves Decision Journey continuity.
